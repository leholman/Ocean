I'm interested in biodiversity change across space and time. My work sits between data science and ecology - I apply DNA-based methods to make sense of the oceans. Ultimately my goal is more healthy future seas, driven by reconstructing prehistoric baseline data, developing advanced ecosystem health tools, and through a curiosity-driven research program.
## Reconstructing Ancient Oceans using ancient eDNA
What can we learn about the climate-culture-biodiversity nexus from ancient marine eDNA? When and where do humans have the largest impact? How can climate interact with these impacts? Is biodiversity a result or a driver of anthropogenic and climatic change?
