How can science inspire and develop new artistic research and works? How can art help science be understood, or generate entirely new ways of thinking in science?

I'm fascinated by these questions and work to integrate artists and artistic perspectives into my science.

For example, in 2023 and 2024 Rhoda Ting and Mikkel Bojesen explored our eDNA data from the [Skagerrak](https://doi.org/10.1111/1755-0998.14086) to produce a series of speculative [sculptures](https://studiothinkinghand.com/works/we-are-all-hybrids.html) and [public works](https://studiothinkinghand.com/public-works/vaesner.html) that question the nature of species and ask how new life may emerge in the future.

More recently, I have been developing [SPARK](https://globe.ku.dk/about/spark-art-science-initiative/), an art–science initiative at the Globe Institute run in collaboration with [Art Hub Copenhagen](https://arthubcopenhagen.net/en/). It brings together artists and scientists to develop new artistic works that help communicate science, ask new questions, and develop new ways of understanding.
